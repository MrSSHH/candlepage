Candlepage

PDF dark mode for Chrome

Dark PDFs. Pictures keep their colors.

Most dark mode tools flip every pixel, so photos and diagrams turn into negatives. Candlepage darkens the page, keeps your pictures real, finds PDFs on web pages and reads scanned books offline, so you can read at 2am without squinting.

    Built for long reading sessions

    Pictures stay true

    Photos, charts and book covers keep their original colors. Choose Off, Auto or All, and see a live preview on your own PDF.

    Auto mode for screenshots

    A light app window in your PDF becomes a dark one, like its own dark mode, while real photos stay untouched.

    Finds PDFs on any page

    Open the popup on a web page and see every PDF link. Open one in dark mode, or jump to it on the page.

    Offline OCR for scanned books

    Reads real page numbers (even i, ii, iii) and builds chapters from the contents page. It runs on your computer and nothing is uploaded.

    Save any picture

    Right-click a picture, drag a frame over the whole image (even when the PDF splits it) and save it dark or as printed.

    Hebrew and English

    The interface works in both languages, including right-to-left layout.

    Questions

    How do I get dark mode on PDF files saved on my computer?

    Chrome blocks extensions from local files by default. Open chrome://extensions, find Candlepage, click Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs. After that, PDFs you open from your computer get dark mode too.

    Will photos and charts turn into negatives?

    No. Candlepage darkens the page and keeps pictures, charts and book covers in their original colors.

    Is Candlepage free?

    Yes, it is a free Chrome extension. If it helps you, you can support the project on Ko-fi.
