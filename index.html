Candlepage

PDF dark mode for Chrome

Dark PDFs. Pictures keep their colors.

Most dark mode tools flip every pixel, so photos and diagrams turn into negatives. Candlepage darkens the page and leaves the images alone, so you can read at 2am without squinting.
